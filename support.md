# Support

<p class="lede">Most answers are below. For anything else, open an issue and we will reply there.</p>

<p class="note"><strong>Get help:</strong> <a href="https://github.com/Liniker-Seixas/netcord/issues/new">open an issue on GitHub</a>. Issues are public, so please don't post health data or anything personal. Do include your iPhone, watch and iOS version.</p>

## How do I try Netcord without a match?

On the Home tab, tap Load demo data: twelve realistic sessions fill every screen. For Court Cam, open it and turn your iPhone sideways (or tap Rotate the screen), then tap Watch a demo match. You can remove the demo data later in Settings, Data, Remove demo data.

## How do I set up Court Cam?

Fix your iPhone to the back fence behind the middle of the baseline, in landscape, as high as you can, with the whole court in view. Choose singles or doubles, tap the four landmarks Court Cam asks for until the guide lines sit on the court lines (use Near service line in the calibration menu, the sliders button, if the near corners are out of shot), then Start tracking. Video is never recorded.

## Court Cam doesn't know which player I am

With an Apple Watch running Netcord, Court Cam finds you from your shots within a few points. Without a watch in singles, the player on the camera's side is taken as you after 20 seconds. In doubles, or to change it, tap yourself on the court.

## My Apple Watch misses shots

Wear the watch on your racket hand and check Settings, Hitting hand. If soft shots are missed, raise Sensitivity in Settings, Shot detection. The watch asks for Health access the first time you start a match: allow it, or shots, heart rate and calories can't be recorded.

## How do I keep score?

On the Apple Watch, tap your side or theirs on the score page, or say "Hey Siri, Netcord point us" (it works mid-rally and from the watch face). Without a watch, use Home, Score a match on the iPhone. Every scorer knows tiebreaks, match tiebreaks, no-ad, break points and change of ends.

## How do I use Netcord with a Garmin?

Through Apple Health. In Garmin Connect, turn on sharing with Apple Health. Then in Netcord open Settings, Garmin & other watches, and turn on Import from Apple Health. Your Garmin tennis workouts then appear in Netcord as sessions with heart rate and calories, or add those to a match you filmed or scored at the same time.

Connect your Garmin, in the same place, links a Garmin directly. It needs Netcord's Connect IQ watch app, which is in private testing and not in Garmin's Connect IQ Store yet.

## Testing Netcord's Garmin app?

Matches arrive while Netcord is open on your iPhone. If it was closed, the Garmin keeps the match: open Netcord on your iPhone, then open Netcord on the Garmin (its summary screen counts) and the match follows within a moment. Some Garmins turn touch off during activities: turn it on in the watch's System settings, or press START and choose Point to you or Point to them.

## How do I bring in workouts from other watches?

In Settings, Garmin & other watches, turn on Import from Apple Health and allow access. Tennis workouts that Garmin Connect or other apps save to Apple Health then appear in Netcord, or add heart rate and calories to the match they record. In Garmin Connect, turn on sharing with Apple Health first. Imported workouts stay on this iPhone and don't go to iCloud; turn the import on on a new iPhone to bring them in again.

## How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you're signed in to iCloud, Sync with iCloud is on by default: your matches, Court Cam maps, settings, profile (all but gender, birth year and height) and photo sync through your own iCloud, and a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID gets them back when Netcord opens. Matches live in Netcord's own private folder in your iCloud Drive, which the Files app doesn't show, so iCloud Drive needs to be on. Heart rate, calories and distance come back from your Apple Watch's workouts in Apple Health; a Garmin match gets them back only if Garmin Connect shares its workouts with Apple Health and Import from Apple Health is on. Step counts don't come back. Without iCloud, Settings, iCloud & backup, Back up everything makes one file, and Restore from a backup reads it on the new iPhone.

## How do I export my data?

Settings, iCloud & backup, Back up everything saves your sessions, profile, photo and settings as one JSON file. Save it wherever you like, for example iCloud Drive.

## How do I delete my data?

Deleting a session removes it, with its Court Cam recording, from this iPhone and, with sync on, from your iCloud and from your other iPhones with sync on. Settings, Data, Delete all sessions does that for every session, including ones in iCloud not yet on this iPhone. Deleting the app doesn't remove Netcord's copies in your iCloud: turn off Sync with iCloud first (Netcord asks before removing them). Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.

## Is Netcord a medical device?

No. Netcord is a sports tracker. Heart rate and other readings are for training insight only, not for diagnosing, treating or monitoring any medical condition.
