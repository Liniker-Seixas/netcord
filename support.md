# Support

<p class="lede">Most answers are below. For anything else, open an issue and we will reply there.</p>

<p class="note"><strong>Get help:</strong> <a href="https://github.com/Liniker-Seixas/netcord/issues/new">open an issue on GitHub</a>. Issues are public, so describe the problem with your iPhone, watch and iOS version, but never post health data or anything personal.</p>

## How do I try Netcord without a match?

On the Home tab, tap Load demo data: twelve realistic matches fill every screen. Court Cam has Watch a demo match on its first screen. You can remove the demo data later in Settings, Data.

## How do I set up Court Cam?

Fix your iPhone to the back fence behind the middle of the baseline, in landscape, as high as you can, with the whole court in view. Choose singles or doubles, tap the four landmarks Court Cam asks for until the guide lines sit on the court lines (use Near service line in the settings menu if the near corners are out of shot), then Start tracking. Video is never recorded.

## Court Cam doesn't know which player I am

With an Apple Watch or a Garmin running Netcord, Court Cam finds you from your shots within a few points. Without a watch in singles, the player on the camera's side is taken as you after 20 seconds. In doubles, or to change it, tap yourself on the court.

## My Apple Watch misses shots

Wear the watch on your racket hand and check Settings, Hitting hand. If soft shots are missed, raise Sensitivity in Settings, Shot detection. The watch asks for Health access the first time you start a match: allow it, or shots, heart rate and calories can't be recorded.

## How do I keep score?

On the Apple Watch, tap your side or theirs on the score page, or say "Hey Siri, Netcord point us". On a Garmin, tap your row or theirs on a touchscreen, or press UP or DOWN; START opens a menu with Point to you and Point to them. Without a watch, use Home, Score a match on the iPhone. Every scorer knows tiebreaks, match tiebreaks, no-ad, break points and change of ends.

## How do I use Netcord with a Garmin?

Install Netcord on the Garmin, then in Netcord on your iPhone open Settings, Garmin & other watches, Connect your Garmin, and choose your watch in Garmin Connect. The Garmin must be paired with that same iPhone in Garmin Connect. Netcord sends its settings (rules, hand, sensitivity) to the watch whenever it connects.

## Taps on my Garmin don't score

Some Garmins turn touch off during activities. Turn it on in the watch's System settings, or press START and choose Point to you or Point to them.

## How do I bring in workouts from other watches?

In Settings, Garmin & other watches, turn on Import from Apple Health and allow access. Tennis workouts that Garmin Connect or other apps save to Apple Health then appear in Netcord, or add heart rate and calories to the match they record. In Garmin Connect, turn on sharing with Apple Health first.

## How do I move to a new iPhone?

Settings, iCloud & backup, Back up everything saves your sessions, profile and settings as one file; on the new iPhone use Restore from a backup. Quick Start and iCloud device backups carry Netcord along too.

## How do I delete my data?

Settings, Data, Delete all sessions removes your matches; deleting the app removes everything it stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

## Is Netcord a medical device?

No. Netcord is a sports tracker. Heart rate and other readings are for training insight only, not for diagnosing, treating or monitoring any medical condition.
